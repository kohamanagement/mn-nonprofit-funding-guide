    Printable one-page guides

    One page per cause, for libraries, community centers and workshops. Pick a cause, add your county to list local funders, choose English, Spanish, Hmong or Somali, then print or save as PDF.

    Spanish, Hmong and Somali versions are draft translations. Please have a native speaker review them before printing copies to hand out. Funder and program names stay in English so people can find them online.

    Start a nonprofit in Minnesota

    The state and IRS steps in order, with fees and timelines. Government fees total about $370 to $715.
